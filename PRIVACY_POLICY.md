# Privacy Policy for Portscape Camera

Last updated: March 31, 2026

Portscape Camera ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy.

## Overview

Portscape Camera is designed to work without accounts, ads, or tracking.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or sell personal information through the app.

The app does not require account creation and does not include third-party advertising SDKs.

## Camera and Photo Library Access

Portscape Camera requests the following permissions only to provide core app functionality:

- Camera access: to capture video.
- Photo Library add access: to save recordings to your Photos library.

Video files you create are saved on your device and managed by Apple Photos based on your device settings.

## Analytics and Tracking

- No cross-app tracking.
- No third-party advertising trackers.
- No user profiling for ads.

## Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal data with data brokers or advertisers.

## Data Retention

Because we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is no server-side personal data retention by us.

Any media you capture is stored locally on your device unless you choose to sync via your own iCloud/Apple services.

## Children's Privacy

Portscape Camera does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at this URL and become effective when published.
